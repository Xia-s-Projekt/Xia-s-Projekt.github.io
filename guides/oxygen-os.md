## Prerequisites
- Unlocked Bootloader
- Custom Recovery (TWRP/OrangeFox)
- Have a brain

## Steps
1. Reboot to recovery.
2. Flash the ROM zip.
3. Format Data (type `yes`).
4. Reboot to System.

## Dev Notes
Ignore any failed to mount red text, those are not failure nor errors.
